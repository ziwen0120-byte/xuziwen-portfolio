# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, design leads, and collaborators reviewing Xu Ziwen's UI/UX portfolio on desktop and mobile. They need to understand experience, design judgment, AI workflow capability, project quality, and contact paths quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present a cinematic personal portfolio that makes design ability visible through motion, hierarchy, embedded prototypes, case work, and concise professional context. Success means a visitor can quickly form trust, inspect relevant work, and contact Xu Ziwen.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, precise, practical. The site should feel visually memorable while still helping hiring reviewers scan important information without friction.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic template portfolios, flat card grids, overdecorated glass effects, cramped oversized typography, clipped content, and motion that hides information or makes scanning harder.

## Design Principles

Show capability through real artifacts and interactions.
Keep cinematic motion readable and reversible.
Let navigation support the narrative without stealing focus.
Use hierarchy and spacing to make recruiter scanning efficient.
Keep responsive layouts stable, legible, and free of overflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve readable contrast, avoid content that depends only on animation, and keep all contact and navigation targets keyboard accessible.
